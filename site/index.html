How the desk works

Both clauses use the same site and per-storm limit. Each pays once when an archived tropical-storm or hurricane center falls inside the radius and the storm’s maximum sustained wind meets the threshold at that same fix. Equality counts.

Distances use a spherical great-circle calculation. A line on the map connects recorded fixes for orientation. Only recorded fixes enter the calculation. Maximum storm wind can differ sharply from wind experienced at the site.

The trigger breakpoint is the smallest radius that includes a sufficiently intense fix for the selected storm. The annual ledger includes seasons with zero triggers. Historical counts describe this archive and rule only. A damage or premium estimate requires additional evidence.

Contracts here are hypothetical. Real instruments can use pressure grids, loss declarations, event-specific data vintages or other rules. The desk uses revised NOAA best tracks from the 1990–2025 Atlantic archive. The export contains both rules, source metadata and storm-level evidence.
